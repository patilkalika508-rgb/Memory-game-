How to Play
  • Players take turns — X goes first, then O.
  • Click an empty cell on the 3D board to place your mark.
  • First to get 3 marks in a row (horizontally, vertically, or diagonally) wins.
  • If all 9 cells fill with no winner, it's a draw.
  • Click "New Round" to play again — the score keeps tallying.
